03
Reading · B1+

Older, but wiser?

Read the article. Hover over the underlined words to see a Russian translation.

We like to believe that wisdom arrives automatically with age. The truth is less comfortable: some people grow older and wiser, while many simply grow older. Living through something once is no guarantee that you will handle it better the next time. Experience can sharpen our judgement — but it can just as easily lock us into fixed habits and quietly feed our overconfidence.

“Wisdom is the idea of a practical use of reason.”— Immanuel Kant

Wisdom is also surprisingly hard to recognise. Intelligence can be demonstrated and knowledge can be displayed, but wisdom often looks like hesitation rather than decisiveness, or silence rather than speech. Socrates distrusted anyone who was certain about everything. Aristotle drew a line between knowing facts and judging well. And for Kant, wisdom meant using reason in real life, where every choice carries consequences.

Modern culture, with its pressure to react instantly and defend opinions loudly, hardly rewards any of this. Wisdom demands the opposite: the courage to pause, to admit “I don’t know”, and to revise your views when the facts change. It is quiet, easily missed, and often only recognised in retrospect — which is perhaps why Kierkegaard observed that life can only be understood backwards, though it must be lived forwards.

06
Story time · Aesop

The Donkey in the Lion’s Skin

One day, a donkey found a lion’s skin on the ground. Hunters had left it there to dry in the sun. The donkey put the skin on and walked into the village. Everybody ran away — people and animals were afraid of the “terrible lion”!

The donkey was very happy. He felt strong and important for the first time in his life. He was so proud that he lifted his head and shouted with joy. But of course, a donkey cannot roar like a lion. Out came a loud, silly “Hee-haw!”

A fox heard the sound and laughed: “Ah! You almost frightened me too — but then I heard your voice.”

Your task: which sentence is the real moral of the story? Choose one.
